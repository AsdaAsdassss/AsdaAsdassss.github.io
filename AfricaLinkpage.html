Welcome to the Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, and Taranguire Wildlife sanctuary.

Tanzania is a world of great beauty. A world of day-to-day survival. The Serengeti is over 5000 square miles of changing plains and Kopjes (islands of rock). The Ngorongoro crater is a wonder of the world. Continuous crater walls thousands of feet high surround a secret and unique sanctuary of African wildlife. Taranguire is one of the largest wildlife parks in Africa. Taranguire is famous for its elephant population. See the beauty of Lions eye to eye, Cheetahs on the hunt, and Elephants on guard.
