We truly do not understand, gain
appropriate respect,

or admiration for Elephants until we visit them where they live. They are the road makers on the Serengeti plains; they are the nomadic families that move across the plains, up and down the rivers, and through the woodlands, making their mark on the land. No other inhabitant of the plains has had as great an impact on the land, and their environment as the Elephant. Their intelligence, family oriented culture, and status are only realized when you spend time with them in their natural habitat. They are truly, the royal families of the African plains. Enjoy your time viewing this amazing royalty of the Serengeti
