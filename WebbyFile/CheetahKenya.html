Cheetahs- Divas of the Plains

Lying out atop the Land Rover on my stomach, they were laying directly below me, just a few feet away, trying to rest and keep out of the heat of the day. Cheetahs like wide open, vast plains. The Masai Mara provides them a preferred habitat where the migration comes in April and May, and leaves again in August, September and even into October.
