Lighthouses have always intrigued me.

Each has their own story, their own place in history, and they are endeared by many. From Door County coastline to Split Rock on Superior. . The term Beacon comes from the original light that was developed in France that used a whole series of lenses that looked like prisms and jewels. Lighthouses inspire almost a spiritual beauty, and ambience. Lighthouses inspire a poetry all of their own.
