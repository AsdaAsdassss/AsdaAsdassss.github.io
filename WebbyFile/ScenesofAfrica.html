In this section you will see different landscape pictures,
and broad perspective views, of Tanzania.

From the vast plains of the Serengeti to the unique ecology of the Ngorongoro crated and on to Taranguire Widlife park. You will see different wide-angle shots attempting to capture the vastness, and broad views of different environments. On my next trip I’ll be looking for Rhinos and Leopards. Rhinos are probably the most rare species to view in their own natural habitat since their numbers are few. Leopards are the most difficult to view and find close opportunities to photograph because of their reclusive and solitary nature.
