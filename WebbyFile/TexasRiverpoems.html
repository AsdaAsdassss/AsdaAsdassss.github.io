The poetry of Texas River Times tells stories of two people, lovers, who moved to Texas to start their lives over. Texas, the Hill Country, is a beautiful, but tough land populated by sincere, resilient and tough people. Straightforward, hard working and very faithful, they view life and the land they live in with a special compassion.
